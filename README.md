# RightsPocket
A student-friendly platform for legal awareness, cyber safety guidance, and complaint draft generation. 


**OPCODE IMPACT 2026 | Hackathon Submission**

**Team ID:** OPC039

## 1. Problem Statement
Many university students lack awareness of their legal rights and do not know how to respond to cyber-related issues such as online payment fraud, cyberbullying, social media impersonation, and privacy violations. Finding reliable legal information and understanding official complaint procedures can be difficult for students. RightsPocket aims to make essential legal awareness and cyber-safety guidance easier to understand and access.

## 2. Solution Title
RightsPocket – is a Student-Friendly Legal Awareness and Cyber-Safety Platform

## 3. Solution Description
RightsPocket is a web-based platform designed to help students understand common legal and cyber-safety issues through simple, accessible guidance. It provides issue-based information, practical steps to follow, links to official reporting resources, and quick-learning cards that encourage safer online behaviour. The platform also includes a complaint-draft generator that helps users prepare a basic draft describing their incident. By presenting information in clear language, RightsPocket aims to help students identify appropriate next steps and access relevant resources more easily.
